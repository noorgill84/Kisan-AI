# Rice Blast Disease (Magnaporthe oryzae)

**Overview:**
Rice blast is a major fungal disease caused by *Magnaporthe oryzae* (Pyricularia oryzae). It can infect leaves, leaf collars, nodes, panicles, and grain stems.

**Symptoms:**
- Leaf blast: Spindle-shaped or diamond-shaped lesions with grayish-white centers and dark brown/reddish margins.
- Lesions enlarge and coalesce, causing entire leaf blades to wither and dry.
- Neck blast: Brown to blackish lesions at the panicle neck, causing panicles to fall over or remain unfilled ("whiteheads").

**Favorable Conditions:**
- Cool temperatures (20–25°C), high humidity (>90%), long dew periods, high soil nitrogen.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Avoid high nitrogen fertilization.
2. Apply systemic fungicides like Tricyclazole 75 WP (0.6 g/L) or Isoprothiolane 40 EC (1.5 mL/L) at first symptom appearance.
3. Maintain field moisture; avoid prolonged dry spells during vegetative stage.
