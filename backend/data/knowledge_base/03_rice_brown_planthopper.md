# Brown Plant Hopper in Rice (Nilaparvata lugens)

**Overview:**
Brown Plant Hopper (BPH) is a sap-sucking insect pest of rice crops.

**Symptoms:**
- Insects cluster at the base of rice tillers near the water level.
- Infested leaves turn yellow-orange, then brown, dry up, and appear scorched ("hopperburn").
- Circular patches of dried plants appear in the field, expanding outward.
- Sooty mold growth develops on honeydew secreted by insects on lower stems.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Drain irrigation water from the field for 3–4 days to disrupt pest multiplication.
2. Avoid excessive use of synthetic pyrethroids which kill natural predators.
3. Apply recommended insecticides like Triflumezopyrim 10 SC or Pymetrozine 50 WG targeting the base of plants.
