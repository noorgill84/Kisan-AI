# Tomato Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)

**Overview:**
Late blight is a fast-spreading oomycete disease in tomato crops under cool, humid weather.

**Symptoms:**
- Dark, water-soaked irregular spots on leaves, expanding rapidly into large dark brown necrotized areas.
- White fungal sporangial growth on underside of leaves in high moisture.
- Stalks turn dark brown/black; green and ripe fruit develop greasy brown firm rot.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Apply contact fungicides like Mancozeb 75 WP (2.5 g/L) prophylactically before humid weather.
2. Apply systemic fungicides like Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (2 g/L) or Dimethomorph upon disease onset.
3. Avoid overhead sprinklers; ensure wide plant spacing for airflow.
