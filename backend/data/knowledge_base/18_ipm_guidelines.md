# Integrated Pest & Disease Management (IPM) Protocols

**Overview:**
Integrated Pest Management combines biological, mechanical, cultural, and targeted chemical measures for sustainable crop protection.

**General Guidelines:**
1. Practice regular field scouting twice a week.
2. Maintain balanced fertilizer application based on soil test results (avoid N excess).
3. Encourage natural enemies (ladybird beetles, chrysoperla, parasitoid wasps) by avoiding broad-spectrum sprays during early crop stage.
4. When pest intensity exceeds Economic Threshold Level (ETL), use eco-friendly biopesticides (Neem oil 10,000 ppm, *Trichoderma viride*, *Bacillus thuringiensis*) before synthetic chemicals.
5. If confidence in diagnosis is low, consult local agricultural extension officers or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) experts before mass chemical application.
