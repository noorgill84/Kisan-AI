# Maize Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda)

**Overview:**
Fall Armyworm (FAW) is a highly destructive pest attacking maize whorls and tassels.

**Symptoms:**
- Ragged holes and extensive defoliation on leaf whorls.
- Sawdust-like frass (insect excrement) accumulated inside the central whorl.
- Larvae with inverted Y-shaped mark on head capsule visible inside whorls.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Handpick larvae or apply neem cake powder into whorls in early stages.
2. Apply Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC (0.4 mL/L) or Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG directly into plant whorls using hand sprayer.
