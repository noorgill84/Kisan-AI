# Potato Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)

**Overview:**
Potato late blight can wipe out entire potato fields within days if weather remains wet and temperatures are between 15–22°C.

**Symptoms:**
- Pale green to dark water-soaked leaf spots, turning brown to purplish-black.
- White mildew/mold growth visible on lower leaf surfaces during moist mornings.
- Tubers exhibit rusty-brown dry rot extending into flesh under skin.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Destroy volunteer potato plants and infected crop debris.
2. Spray Cymoxanil + Mancozeb (2 g/L) or Infinito (Fluopicolide + Propamocarb) immediately upon fog/humidity alert.
3. Perform proper earthing-up to cover tubers deeply with soil.
