# Potassium Deficiency in Field Crops

**Overview:**
Potassium (K) regulates stomatal opening, water balance, and stress tolerance in plants.

**Symptoms:**
- Marginal leaf scorching or firing (browning along leaf edges) on older leaves.
- Leaves appear burnt at margins while central midrib area remains green.
- Increased susceptibility to lodging (stem breakage) and fungal pathogens.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Apply Muriate of Potash (MOP / KCl) or Potassium Sulfate during land preparation or early dressing.
2. Apply foliar potassium nitrate (KNO3) 1% spray for quick recovery during vegetative phase.
