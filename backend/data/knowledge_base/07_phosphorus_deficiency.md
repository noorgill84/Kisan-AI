# Phosphorus Deficiency in Maize and Wheat

**Overview:**
Phosphorus (P) deficiency severely inhibits root growth and early seedling establishment.

**Symptoms:**
- Dark green to reddish-purple pigmentation along leaf margins and stems (anthocyanin accumulation).
- Delayed maturity, weak root development, slender stalks.
- Stunted overall plant height.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Apply soil phosphorus fertilizer (Single Super Phosphate / DAP) close to root zones during sowing.
2. Maintain soil pH between 6.0–7.5 for optimal phosphorus availability.
