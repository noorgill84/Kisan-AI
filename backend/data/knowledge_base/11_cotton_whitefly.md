# Cotton Whitefly & Leaf Curl Virus (Bemisia tabaci)

**Overview:**
Whiteflies transmit Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV) while sucking sap from undersides of leaves.

**Symptoms:**
- Upward or downward curling of leaf margins, vein thickening, and enations (leaf-like outgrowths) on undersides.
- Honeydew accumulation causing black sooty mold growth on cotton bolls and foliage.
- Stunted plant growth and severely reduced boll formation.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Install yellow sticky traps (15–20/acre) to monitor and capture adult whiteflies.
2. Spray Afidopyropen 50 g/L DC or Diafenthiuron 50 WP targeting leaf undersides.
3. Remove weed hosts near cotton fields.
