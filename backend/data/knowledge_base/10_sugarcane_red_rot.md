# Sugarcane Red Rot (Colletotrichum falcatum)

**Overview:**
Red Rot is known as the "cancer of sugarcane", causing massive yield loss and sugar quality deterioration.

**Symptoms:**
- Third or fourth leaf shows yellowing, drying, and wilting.
- Splitting open affected stalk reveals longitudinal red discoloration interrupted by white transverse bands.
- Alcoholic or sour odor emitted from internal split stalk tissues.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Uproot and burn diseased clumps immediately.
2. Treat setts with Carbendazim (0.1%) or hot water (52°C for 30 minutes) prior to planting.
3. Adopt 2–3 year crop rotation with non-host crops like paddy or legumes.
