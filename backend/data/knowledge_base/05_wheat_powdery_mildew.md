# Wheat Powdery Mildew (Blumeria graminis f. sp. tritici)

**Overview:**
Powdery mildew is a fungal disease affecting wheat leaves, sheaths, and ears in high-density foliage fields.

**Symptoms:**
- White to grayish fluffy talcum-like fungal patches on upper leaf surfaces.
- Patches enlarge, turn dull gray to light brown, with tiny black specks (cleistothecia).
- Affected leaves turn yellow, chlorotic, and die early.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Avoid dense seed sowing rates to promote canopy airflow.
2. Apply sulfur-based or hexaconazole fungicides if infection exceeds threshold level.
