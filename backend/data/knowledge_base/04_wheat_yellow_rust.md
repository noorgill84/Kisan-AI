# Wheat Yellow Rust / Stripe Rust (Puccinia striiformis f. sp. tritici)

**Overview:**
Yellow rust (stripe rust) is a devastating fungal disease of wheat crops in cooler agricultural zones.

**Symptoms:**
- Bright yellow to orange pustules arranged in linear stripes/stripes parallel to leaf veins.
- Pustules rupture leaf epidermis releasing yellow powdery urediniospores.
- Leaves dry prematurely, reducing grain filling and yield dramatically.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Monitor field early during tillering and jointing stages.
2. Apply systemic triazole fungicide like Propiconazole 25 EC (1 mL/L) or Tebuconazole upon first detection of stripes.
3. Grow resistant wheat cultivars recommended by local agricultural extension.
