# Environmental Stress: Waterlogging & Heat/Drought Scorching

**Overview:**
Abiotic environmental factors such as prolonged water stagnation or extreme heat waves cause physiological damage mimicking disease.

**Symptoms:**
- Waterlogging: Yellowing and dropping of lower leaves, root decay (black, sour roots), wilted leaves despite abundant soil water.
- Heat/Drought stress: Tip burn, leaf rolling, floral drop, sunscald on fruits, brown crispy leaf margins.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Dig surface field trenches to drain excess water within 24 hours of heavy rains.
2. Apply light frequent irrigation or micro-sprinklers during heat waves.
3. Apply foliar potassium nitrate (1%) or salicylic acid to enhance crop stress resilience.
