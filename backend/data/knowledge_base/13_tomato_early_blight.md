# Tomato Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

**Overview:**
Early blight affects older lower leaves first and moves upward in warm temperatures with dew or rain.

**Symptoms:**
- Concentric ring lesions ("target spot" pattern) on lower leaves with dark brown borders.
- Leaves yellow around lesions, drop prematurely, exposing fruit to sunscald.
- Dark sunken lesions with concentric rings at stem ends of fruits.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Mulch soil surface to prevent soil splash onto lower leaves.
2. Spray Chlorothalonil 75 WP (2 g/L) or Azoxystrobin (1 mL/L) at 7–10 day intervals.
