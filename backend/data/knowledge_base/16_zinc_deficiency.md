# Micronutrient Zinc Deficiency in Rice ("Khaira") and Wheat

**Overview:**
Zinc deficiency is extremely widespread in calcareous, alkaline, or continuously submerged paddy soils.

**Symptoms:**
- In rice ("Khaira" disease): 2–3 weeks after transplanting, younger leaves develop rusty-brown bronze spots that coalesce covering the leaf.
- In wheat and maize: White to light yellow bands on both sides of midrib on upper leaves ("white bud").
- Severe growth stunting and delayed crop maturity.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Apply Zinc Sulfate heptahydrate (21% Zn @ 25 kg/ha) during basal soil preparation.
2. Spray 0.5% Zinc Sulfate + 0.25% Lime solution as a foliar application twice at 10-day intervals.
