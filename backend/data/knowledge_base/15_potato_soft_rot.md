# Potato Blackleg and Soft Rot (Pectobacterium / Dickeya spp.)

**Overview:**
Bacterial soft rot and blackleg affect potato haulms and harvested tubers in warm, wet soils.

**Symptoms:**
- Inky black slimy rot at the base of stems (blackleg) originating from infected seed tubers.
- Stunted growth, yellowing upper leaves, wilting and collapse of main stems.
- Tubers turn soft, watery, and produce foul-smelling liquid breakdown.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Use certified disease-free seed tubers; avoid cutting tubers before planting if soil is wet.
2. Plant in well-drained soils; avoid excessive over-irrigation.
3. Allow tuber skin to harden in dry soil before harvesting.
