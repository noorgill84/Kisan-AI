# Nitrogen Deficiency in Field Crops (Wheat, Rice, Maize)

**Overview:**
Nitrogen (N) is a mobile macro-nutrient essential for chlorophyll production and vegetative growth.

**Symptoms:**
- General yellowing (chlorosis) starting in older lower leaves from tip downward along midrib ("V-shaped" yellowing in maize).
- Stunted growth, thin stems, pale green young leaves.
- Premature senescense of lower foliage and poor tillering in cereals.

**Management & Decision Support Steps:**
1. Top-dress with Urea or ammonium nitrate dissolved in irrigation water.
2. Apply foliar spray of 1–2% Urea solution for rapid canopy absorption.
3. Test soil organic carbon and adjust basal N application rates.
